## The Email Template

Use this for all Priority 1 and Priority 2 contacts. Personalize the `[bracketed]` fields.

---

**Subject:** Quick favor — refreshing my speaker site

---

Hi `[First Name]`,

I'm rebuilding my speaker website and I'd love to include the kind words you shared after `[event name / our work together at Company]`. Would you be comfortable if I attributed this quote to you by name and title?

> "`[paste their exact quote]`"

If you'd prefer to tweak the wording or update your title, I'm happy to use whatever version you're most comfortable with. Either way, thank you — it meant a lot then and still does.

Warm regards,
Scott

---

### Why This Template Works at the $20k Tier

1. **It doesn't ask for a new testimonial.** Asking someone to *write* something is a big ask. Asking them to *claim* something they already said is a small ask. Conversion rate is dramatically higher.
2. **It gives them an edit option.** Senior executives are protective of their public statements. Offering them control over the wording removes the friction of "what if it doesn't sound like me anymore?"
3. **It's three sentences.** Busy people respond to short emails. No preamble. No explanation of why testimonials matter. Just: here's the quote, can I use your name, you can edit it. Done.

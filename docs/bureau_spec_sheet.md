## 4. Logistics

### Travel

| Field                  | Detail                                                    |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Base                   | Singapore                                                 |
| Availability           | Global. All time zones. 48-week calendar.                |
| Travel class           | Business class for flights over 4 hours. Economy for under 4 hours. |

| Visa requirements      | Australian passport. Most markets visa-free or visa-on-arrival. Client responsible for any required invitation letters. |
| Travel days            | One travel day before event. One travel day after. No same-day arrivals for keynotes. |
| Accommodation          | 4-star minimum. Quiet room. Late checkout on departure day. |
| Ground transport       | Private transfer airport-to-hotel and hotel-to-venue. No shared shuttles. |
| Per diem               | Not required. Meals during event covered by client.       |
| Travel companions      | Scott travels solo unless a workshop requires a facilitator. |

### Technical — Audio/Visual Requirements

> **Principle:** The audience should hear every word and see every pixel.
> No exceptions. No compromises. Test everything the day before.

| Requirement            | Specification                                             |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Microphone             | **Wireless lavalier (DPA 4088 or equivalent)** as primary. Wireless handheld as backup on stage. No podium mic. Scott moves. |
| Monitors               | Confidence monitor (presenter view) at stage front, angled toward speaker position. Minimum 40" at 10ft distance. |
| Projection             | Minimum 1080p. 16:9 aspect ratio. Laser projector preferred. No rear-projection unless venue-mandated. Screen must be visible from all seats. |
| Slides                 | Scott provides final deck in 16:9 PDF and Keynote format, 48 hours before event. No PowerPoint conversion without approval. |
| Slide clicker          | Logitech Spotlight or equivalent. Scott carries a backup. |
| Stage lighting         | Front wash. No colored gels. No moving heads. Clean white or warm white only. Scott must be visible to the back row and to cameras simultaneously. |
| Stage                  | Open stage. No podium. No lectern. Minimum 20ft wide, 12ft deep. Hardwood or matte-finish preferred. No carpet. |
| Recording              | Client may record audio and video for internal use only. External distribution, broadcast, or social media posting requires separate written agreement. |
| Internet               | Hardwired ethernet backup at tech desk. WiFi for presenter laptop is not acceptable as primary connection if slides contain live demos. |
| Sound check            | Minimum 30-minute sound check required, scheduled no less than 2 hours before audience entry. |

# Stage Intro Script

> **For:** The person introducing Scott on stage.
> **Duration:** 60 seconds.
> **Tone:** Low-frequency. Deliberate. The cadence of someone delivering facts, not hype.
> **Delivery note:** Read this slowly. Let the dates land. Let the pauses do the work.

---

## Rules for the MC

1. **Do not say** "futurist," "keynote speaker," "guru," "thought leader," "visionary," or any variation.
2. **Do not ad-lib** additional qualifiers before or after Scott's name.
3. **Do not rush.** The script is paced with intentional beats. Honor them.
4. The intro ends on his name. His name is the credential. Nothing follows it.

---

## The Script

On June 29, 2007, Apple launched the iPhone.

That date created a consumer who had never existed before — one who would
carry a computer in his pocket, demand transparency from every institution,
and walk away from any experience that failed to meet him on his terms.

His name is Isaac.

On November 30, 2022, OpenAI launched ChatGPT.

That date created a generation that will never know a world before
artificial intelligence — one that will not interact with your brand
directly, will not compare your service manually, and will delegate
every decision to an agent that has no loyalty and no memory of your
company's name.

Her name is Esther.

*(pause)*

Our next speaker has spent twenty years between those two dates.

He built financial systems in Cambodia for people who had never held
a bank account. He served as Vice President at Visa. He helped build
the world's first downloadable bank. He has advised Microsoft, Google,
and Standard Chartered on the shifts that are rewriting their industries.

He wrote two best-selling books about what happens when technology
moves faster than the institutions that depend on it.

And then his son Jenson — a boy who could not use the technology
his father spent a career building — changed how he sees everything.

*(pause)*

Jenson is why Isaac matters.
Jenson is why Esther's world will refuse to exclude.

He is the architect of the Generational Pincer.

Please welcome Scott Bales.

## Delivery Notes

| Beat | Instruction |
|------|-------------|
| "June 29, 2007" | State the date like a fact. No excitement. A historian's register. |
| "His name is Isaac." | Pause after. Let the audience absorb the naming. |
| "November 30, 2022" | Same register. The parallel structure should land without the MC emphasizing it. |
| "Her name is Esther." | Pause after. Longer than the Isaac pause. |
| "twenty years between those two dates" | This is the hinge. The audience shifts from concept to person. Slow down. |
| "Cambodia... Visa... first downloadable bank" | Three facts. Three beats. No connective tissue needed. Let each one land. |
| "And then his son Jenson" | Drop the volume slightly. This is not dramatic — it is private. The audience should lean in. |
| "changed how he sees everything" | Full stop. Do not rush to the next line. |
| "Jenson is why Isaac matters." | Resume normal volume. This is declarative. |
| "Jenson is why Esther's world will refuse to exclude." | Hold. |
| "the Generational Pincer" | This phrase should feel like a proper noun. No hesitation. The MC owns it. |
| "Please welcome Scott Bales." | No qualifier. No "the incredible." No "ladies and gentlemen." Just the name. Applause follows the name, not an adjective. |
